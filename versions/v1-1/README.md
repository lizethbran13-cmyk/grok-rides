# GROK RIDES

A free-roam vehicle sim for phones and computers ("basically Grok Forza").
Drive, fly and float around one big world with a city, a small town, a desert, an icy tundra,
a mountain with switchbacks, a lake and an airfield. Race NPCs or friends, do jobs for money,
buy cars, trucks, a limo, a bus, a monster truck, a boat, a helicopter, a stunt plane and a hot air balloon.

Play: https://lizethbran13-cmyk.github.io/grok-rides/

Online co-op (up to 3) uses the shared Grok Arcade netcode (grok-net.js + PeerJS).

## Walk around and go inside
Stop and tap **🚶 WALK** to get out. Buildings with a glowing green door are places you can enter
(17 of them, in every region): Grok Motors showroom, Sky Field aircraft hangar, Marina boat shop,
two garages (paint & tune), Grok Dispatch job office, Grand Grok Hotel, Race Club HQ, Burger Blast,
Maple Diner, Pine Hollow Pizza, the General Store, two gas stations with mini marts, the Sizzle
Trading Post, the Frostbite Ice Cafe & Research Outpost and the Grokmore Mountain Lodge.
Inside you can talk to people, buy rides, start jobs and races, buy hats and souvenirs, and eat
snacks that give small, short bonuses (switched off during races).
